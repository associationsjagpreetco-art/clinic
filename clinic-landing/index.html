The Problem

What's costing your clinic time and patients

Patients calling repeatedly to check availability
Your reception team handles the same questions all day — when can I come in, what slots are open — instead of focusing on patients who are already there.
Hours lost to manual booking and follow-ups
Entries in registers, messages on WhatsApp, calls to confirm — managing appointments by hand takes time your staff doesn't have.
No-shows leave appointment slots empty
Patients forget or don't bother cancelling. Without reminders, those slots go unfilled and the revenue is simply lost.
No centralized view of what's coming up
Appointments scattered across notebooks, phone notes, and messages mean no one has a clear picture of the day ahead.
What We Build

Your clinic's booking system

The Process

How it works

01

02
